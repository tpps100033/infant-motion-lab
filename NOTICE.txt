Preprocessing and measurements:
Independent JavaScript implementation aligned with the existing Python research
pipeline and RTMPose SimCC coordinate decoding. BGR preprocessing matches rtmlib
0.0.15. Floating-point and browser decoding differences are documented in DEPLOY.md.
rtmlib: https://github.com/Tau-J/rtmlib

The built-in demo consists entirely of generated joint coordinates.
No patient photographs, source videos, or clinical records are included.
